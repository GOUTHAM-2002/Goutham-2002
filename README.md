# 💫 About Me:

🔭 I'm an **AI safety researcher at Dartmouth College** interested in understanding and reducing **loss-of-control risks from advanced AI systems**. <br><br>

🧠 I’m particularly interested in empirical questions around **scheming, model oversight, evaluation awareness, untrusted monitoring, and robustness of safety interventions**. I want to understand when apparently aligned behavior is reliable, when it breaks under distribution shift or adversarial incentives, and what evidence would actually justify trusting a model in deployment. <br><br>

🔬 My research interests sit at the intersection of **AI control, evaluations, and interpretability**. I’m especially interested in using internal model representations and controlled behavioral experiments to study whether models are hiding capabilities, responding strategically to oversight, or behaving differently because they recognize that they are being evaluated. <br><br>

⚙️ I like research questions that can be turned into **concrete threat models and falsifiable experiments**: defining the failure mode, identifying the assumptions under which it matters, constructing an evaluation, stress-testing alternative explanations, and determining whether the result changes how we should monitor or control advanced models. <br><br>

🧪 I’m interested in questions such as whether **monitoring remains reliable against strategically behaving models**, whether safety fine-tuning survives subsequent optimization, how models respond to incentives for deception or goal preservation, and whether interpretability methods can provide useful evidence about dangerous internal computation rather than only explaining benign behavior. <br><br>

🏛️ I'm pursuing an **M.S. in Computer Science (AI Track) at Dartmouth College** in the **Minds, Machines & Society Lab**, advised by **Prof. Soroush Vosoughi**. I’m supported by the **AMD AI Pervasive Research Scholarship**. <br><br>

🔬 In **Summer 2026**, I will be a **Visiting Research Collaborator** with **OPTML Lab / MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab**, studying how optimizer choice affects the robustness and persistence of **LLM unlearning**. <br><br>

💬 Ask me about **AI control, scheming evaluations, evaluation awareness, mechanistic interpretability, untrusted monitoring, safety fine-tuning, model robustness, and empirical AI safety**. <br><br>

📫 How to reach me: **[goutham.nalagatla.gr@dartmouth.edu](mailto:goutham.nalagatla.gr@dartmouth.edu)** <br><br>

⚡ Fun fact: I’m most interested in experiments where the interesting result is not simply that a model fails, but **why the failure changes our confidence in a proposed safety mechanism**.
